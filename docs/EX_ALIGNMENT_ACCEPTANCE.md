## Evidence packet and stopping boundary

For each row record status, execution result, exact candidate identity, profile/configuration hash, test names/results, logs/artifact hashes, run identity and reviewer. For chain-dependent claims include the actual network, authorized source/contract, transaction/block/log and canonicality/confirmation policy. For institutional authenticity identify the approved parser/profile and original-document verification evidence without leaking originals. For live durability include real production-driver/server transaction, concurrency and fault evidence; SQL-capture or schema-only restore does not substitute.

The legacy 24-ID verifiers, I0–I12 mapping and historical 23/24 score remain legacy coverage only. A revised clause inside a legacy ID also needs direct semantic tests; retaining its ID does not grandfather a pass. Full-revision delivery requires the applicable overlay plus baseline evidence. NFR-004, P1–P8 and Stage F prerequisites remain open. No new global gate is imposed on unrelated completed temporal/synthetic slices.
