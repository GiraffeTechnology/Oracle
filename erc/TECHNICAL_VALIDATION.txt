NUMBERING

  ERC numbers are assigned by the editors, sequentially. Authors do not choose
  them and a chosen number is reassigned. The submit tree carries the numeric
  placeholder 9999. A numeric placeholder is required: eipw preamble-eip
  rejects a non-integer, and eipw-action path2number crashes on a filename that
  is not erc-<digits>.md.
